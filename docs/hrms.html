2. GPS Tracking, Live Telemetry & Trip Management

For field engineers, survey teams, and transit vehicles, the system provides real-time location telemetry with automatic map matching and stop analysis:

Background GPS Foreground Service

On native Android/iOS, a persistent background service (background_tracking_service.dart) acquires precise GPS coordinates at configurable intervals, streaming breadcrumb points to the backend even when the device screen is off.

OSRM Map Matching & Gap Detection

Raw GPS breadcrumbs are processed through OSRM / Valhalla map-matching services to snap points to verified roadway centerlines, calculating real traveled kilometers, idling spots, and transit gap intervals.

⚠️
Mid-Trip Session Security: In [auth.service.ts](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/dev/NB-HRMS/backend/src/modules/auth/auth.service.ts), if an employee has an active open trip record (endTime: null), the backend automatically refreshes their JWT and extends Redis session TTL upon polling, ensuring active field workers are never interrupted by standard session expirations while on duty.

3. Payroll & 7th Pay Commission Engine

The payroll system supports institutional 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC) matrices alongside industrial contractor compensation schedules:

Component Calculation Formula / Rule Statutory Status
Basic Pay Assigned from 7th CPC Matrix (Pay Band & Grade Pay index). Taxable Earnings
Dearness Allowance (DA) Basic Pay × DA_Percentage (adjusted bi-annually per government notifications). Taxable Earnings
House Rent Allowance (HRA) Tier-based percentage (X: 24%, Y: 16%, Z: 8% of Basic Pay). Taxable Earnings (Exemption under 10(13A))
Transport Allowance (TA) Fixed monthly allowance graded by employee classification + DA on TA. Taxable Earnings
Provident Fund (PF) 12% of (Basic Pay + DA), capped per statutory guidelines. Statutory Deduction
Employee State Insurance (ESIC) 0.75% Employee, 3.25% Employer for gross salary under statutory threshold. Statutory Deduction
Professional Tax (PT) State-specific progressive slab deduction (e.g. Maharashtra, Gujarat, Delhi). Statutory Deduction
