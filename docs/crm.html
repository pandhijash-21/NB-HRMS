Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

A dual-lifecycle real estate and enterprise CRM powering omni-channel lead generation, automated round-robin sales allocation, PBX telephony, WhatsApp conversational workflows, unit inventory matrices, and post-sales billing.

🎯
Pre-Sales Lead Funnel

Omni-source capture (Meta, Google, 99acres), algorithmic round-robin allocation, and automated lead aging.

📞
CTI Telephony & WhatsApp

Click-to-call PBX integration (Elision / Greeter), automated call recordings, and BotBiz WhatsApp HSM messaging.

🏢
Inventory & Post-Sales

Tower/unit inventory grids, live booking states, milestone payment schedules, and demand notices.

2. CTI Telephony & PBX Integration

The platform interfaces with enterprise Cloud Telephony PBX systems (Elision & Greeter):

Click-to-Call PBX Bridge

Sales reps initiate calls directly from the Flutter app. The backend dispatches an originate command to the PBX server, connecting the agent's extension and ringing the prospect simultaneously.

Automated Call Recordings & In-App Player

When a call completes, the PBX server posts a webhook with duration, disposition, and audio URL. Flutter plays the recording securely via [crm_audio_player_dialog.dart](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/dev/NB-HRMS/nb_crm_flutter/lib/features/crm/presentation/widgets/crm_audio_player_dialog.dart) with seeking and playback speed controls.

3. Real Estate Unit Inventory & Post-Sales Billing

Projects, towers, and individual units are managed with live locking and payment schedules:

Status Color Code Allowed Actions Behavior
AVAILABLE GREEN Quote, Block, Book Open for general sales inquiries and client quotations.
BLOCKED AMBER Unblock, Confirm Booking Temporary token hold (typically 24 to 48 hours) pending payment confirmation.
BOOKED BLUE Generate Schedule, View Billing Customer agreement signed; payment milestones activated.
SOLD PURPLE Handover, Possession, NOC 100% payments cleared; registry and possession completed.
💡
Real-Time Inventory Synchronization: Whenever any unit's status transitions (e.g. from Available to Blocked), the backend broadcasts crm_unit_update over Socket.IO. All sales rep devices viewing the [TowerUnitsScreen](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/dev/NB-HRMS/nb_crm_flutter/lib/features/erp/presentation/screens/tower_units_screen.dart) update their visual grid in real time without refreshing.

4. Channel Partner (Broker) Ecosystem

External brokers register prospects through the Channel Partner portal:

  • Client Protection Lock: When a broker registers a lead, that client is exclusively locked to the broker for 60 to 90 days.
  • Commission Slabs: Configurable commission structures (e.g., 2% for Tier 1, 2.5% for Tier 2) with automated payout voucher generation upon unit registration.
