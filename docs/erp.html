2. Daily Progress Reports (DPR) & Multi-Draft Offline Auto-Save

Site engineers record field conditions directly from the mobile app on active construction sites where connectivity is intermittent:

Site Operations Telemetry
  • Labor Tracking: Mason, carpenter, bar bender, skilled, and helper counts.
  • Machinery Hours: Excavators, tower cranes, transit mixers (working vs. idle hours).
  • Material Consumption: Cement bags, reinforcement steel metric tons, RMC cubic meters.
  • Work Item Progress: Quantities executed against specific BOQ item codes.
Multi-Draft Auto-Save Engine

Powered by [form_draft_service.dart](file:///c:/Users/DELL/Desktop/dev/NB-HRMS/nb_crm_flutter/lib/core/services/form_draft_service.dart):

  • Engineers can maintain multiple concurrent DPR drafts (e.g., Draft for Tower A, Draft for Storm Drain B).
  • Forms automatically save every keystroke to persistent encrypted local storage.
  • Leaving the screen midway or restarting the app automatically restores the exact draft upon returning.

3. Procurement & 3-Way Matching Engine

Prevents inventory shrinkage and over-billing through systematic checks before payment approvals:

Step Document Verification Criteria Approver Role
1 Material Requisition (MR) Verifies requested quantity does not exceed overall BOQ structural estimates. Project Manager / Quantity Surveyor
2 Purchase Order (PO) Approves rates, vendor terms, payment schedules, and delivery milestones. Purchase Head / Director
3 Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Site security and storekeeper physical weighbridge check and visual inspection. Site Storekeeper
4 3-Way Match Verification Auto-reconciliation: PO Rate = Invoice Rate & GRN Quantity = Invoiced Quantity. Finance / Accounts Auditor

4. Work Orders & Subcontractor Billing (RA Bills)

Subcontractors submit Running Account (RA) bills against certified completed milestones:

  • Retention Deduction: Automatic 5% to 10% deduction held until the end of the defect liability period (DLP).
  • Statutory Deductions: TDS under 194C (1% or 2%), GST TDS (2%), and labor cess (1%).
  • Material Recovery: Cost of contractor-issued cement or steel deducted from gross payable bills.
